How it runs

  1. Brief. You send the product, the audience and where the video will run.
  2. Script and storyboard. You approve both, or ask for changes, before any animation.
  3. First cut. A full draft with sound, so you review the real pacing.
  4. Final files. Changes applied, then every format delivered.
